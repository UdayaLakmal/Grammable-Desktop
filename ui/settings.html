Welcome to Grammable
  1. Allow keyboard access. Grammable copies your selection and pastes the result through your desktop's permission. It asks in a window titled Remote Desktop: keep both switches on, leave Remember This Selection ticked, and click Share.
  2. Select text in any app.
  3. Press Ctrl+Alt+G, or click the G button that appears next to it.
  4. Pick an action, review the suggestion, then Replace.
Grammable lives in the system tray. Pick your AI under AI Backends.
